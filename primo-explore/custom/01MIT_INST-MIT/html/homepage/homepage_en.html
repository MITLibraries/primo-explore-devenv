What am I searching?

Articles, Books & More provides one search for books and e-books, journals and articles, databases, media and more, available in the library and online. Learn more about Articles, Books & More.

If you want to limit your search to materials available in the library, choose “MIT library catalog” from the "All" drop-down menu in the search box.

 Other specialized search tools
  • Research Databases: List of online databases, journals, and ebook platforms
  • MIT Open Scholarship: MIT theses, papers, and research outputs
  • Digital Collections: Images, maps, and digitized materials
  • Archives & Manuscripts: Archives and manuscripts in Distinctive Collections
  • Geospatial Data: Geospatial/GIS data
  • MIT Libraries Dataverse: Research datasets
  • Archnet: Architecture and design resources related to the Muslim world
Not available at MIT?
  • Try Borrow Direct to request books from Ivy Plus Libraries.
  • Use Interlibrary Borrowing to request journal articles and book chapters from libraries worldwide.
  • Or you can suggest a purchase for books and e-books.
